Orion Logistics Ltd. Employee Handbook

1. About the Company

This handbook sets out the working rules that apply to all employees of Orion Logistics Ltd. It is updated every September by the People Operations team, and employees are informed of changes by internal email.

Orion Logistics was founded in Mersin in 2015. The company operates warehouses in Mersin and Adana and currently has 210 employees. Its main business is port logistics and cold-chain transport for the food sector.

New employees go through an orientation program that lasts two working days. During the orientation, each new employee visits both warehouses and completes the occupational safety training.

2. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 08:30 to 17:30 on weekdays, with a one-hour lunch break. Core working time is 10:00 to 15:00. Warehouse staff work in shifts, and shift plans are announced every Friday for the following week.

Overtime requires the prior approval of the shift supervisor. Approved overtime is paid at the hourly wage plus twenty-five percent. Time off in lieu of overtime pay is not offered.

3. Leave

Paid annual leave is 20 working days per year for employees in their first three years at the company and 26 working days for employees who have completed three years. Up to 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year; carried-over days are forfeited if they are not used by April 30.

Employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid sick leave per year. For sick leave longer than two days, a doctor's report must be submitted to People Operations.

Marriage leave is 3 working days and paternity leave is 7 working days. Leave requests are entered in the shift planning system at least 5 working days in advance.

4. Remote Work

Office staff may work remotely for at most 2 days a week. Monday is the office day on which remote work is not allowed. Warehouse and shift staff cannot work remotely.

Working remotely from abroad is not allowed. Employees who work remotely receive a monthly home office allowance of 800 TL.

5. Benefits and Expenses

All employees receive a meal card top-up of 380 TL for each working day. The company provides private health insurance for employees and their children under 18; dental treatment is covered up to 4,000 TL per year.

Business expenses must be entered in the expense system no later than 60 days after the date of the expense. Taxi costs are covered for employees who finish a shift after 22:00.

Office staff are given a laptop, which is replaced every 4 years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT team within 48 hours.

6. Leaving the Company

The notice period is 2 weeks for employees with less than one year of service, 4 weeks for those with one to three years, and 6 weeks for those with more than three years. Questions about this handbook can be sent to people@orionlogistics.example.
