Residential Lease Agreement

Article 1 – Parties and Premises

This agreement is made between Ayse Demir, the landlord, and Can Yilmaz, the tenant. The leased property is a 2+1, 95 square meter apartment in the Cankaya district of Ankara, to be used by the tenant as a residence only. The landlord can be reached by phone at 0312 555 0147.

Article 2 – Lease Term

The lease term starts on March 1, 2026 and lasts two years.

Article 3 – Rent and Payment

The monthly rent is 24,000 TL and is paid into the landlord's bank account by the 1st of each month. In case of late payment, a late fee of one percent of the monthly rent is charged for each week of delay.

Article 4 – Deposit

When signing the agreement, the tenant pays a deposit equal to one month's rent, that is, 24,000 TL. The deposit is returned within 15 days of moving out.

Article 5 – Rent Increase

At the end of each lease year, the rent is increased by 25 percent.

Article 6 – Expenses

Electricity, water, natural gas and internet costs are paid by the tenant. The building maintenance fee and property tax are paid by the landlord.

Article 7 – Rules of Use

The tenant may keep pets on the premises with the written permission of the landlord. Subletting the premises is prohibited.

Article 8 – Termination

If the tenant wants to terminate the agreement, they must give the landlord written notice at least 1 month in advance.

Article 9 – Disputes

The courts of Ankara have jurisdiction over disputes arising from this agreement.
