Residential Lease Agreement

Article 1 – Parties and Premises

This agreement is made under the following terms between Mehmet Arslan, the landlord, on the one hand, and Elif Kaya, the tenant, on the other. The leased property is a 3+1, 125 square meter apartment located in the Bostanli neighborhood of the Karsiyaka district of Izmir, and it will be used by the tenant as a residence only.

Article 2 – Lease Term

The lease term starts on September 1, 2026 and lasts one year. Unless the tenant gives written notice at least 15 days before the end of the term, the agreement is deemed renewed for one year under the same terms.

Article 3 – Rent and Payment

The monthly rent is 32,500 TL. The rent is paid in advance into the landlord's bank account by the 5th of each month. In case of late payment of the rent, a late fee of five per thousand of the monthly rent is charged for each day of delay. A tenant who fails to pay the rent for two consecutive months is served a formal notice by the landlord.

Article 4 – Deposit

When signing the agreement, the tenant pays a deposit equal to two months' rent, that is, 65,000 TL. The deposit is returned to the tenant within 30 days of moving out, provided that the premises are handed over undamaged at the end of the agreement and all debts have been paid. No interest is paid on the deposit.

Article 5 – Rent Increase

At the end of each lease year, the rent is increased by the twelve-month average of the consumer price index (CPI) for the previous lease year. The increase rate may not exceed legal limits.

Article 6 – Rules of Use

The tenant may not keep pets on the premises. Without the written permission of the landlord, the tenant may not lease the premises to another person, sublet them or make alterations to them. Smoking on the premises is prohibited.

Article 7 – Expenses

Electricity, water, natural gas and internet costs, as well as the building maintenance fee, are paid by the tenant. Property tax and the costs of major structural repairs to the building are paid by the landlord.

Article 8 – Termination

If the tenant wants to terminate the agreement, they must give the landlord written notice at least 2 months in advance. If the premises are vacated without observing the notice period, the tenant must pay the rent corresponding to the notice period.

Article 9 – Disputes

The courts and enforcement offices of Izmir have jurisdiction over disputes arising from this agreement. The agreement has been drawn up in two copies and signed by the parties after reading it.
