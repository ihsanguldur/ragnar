Atlas Software Inc. Employee Handbook

1. Introduction and About the Company

This handbook was prepared to bring together, in one place, the rights, responsibilities and internal practices that apply to all employees of Atlas Software Inc. The rules in this handbook are considered an integral part of the employment contract and are reviewed every January by the Human Resources department.

Atlas Software was founded in Izmir in 2011. The company headquarters is in Bayrakli, Izmir, and the company also has a development office in Ankara. The company currently has 340 employees and mainly develops enterprise software solutions for the logistics and retail sectors.

Every new employee goes through an orientation program on their first working day. The orientation program lasts three working days, and during this time the employee is assigned a "buddy". The buddy is responsible for answering the employee's questions during their first three months.

In situations not explicitly covered by this handbook, the applicable labor legislation and the employment contract signed by the employee apply. Employees are informed about changes to the handbook by internal email.

2. Working Hours

Standard working hours at our company are 09:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. The lunch break is one hour, from 12:30 to 13:30, and is not counted as working time. Total weekly working time is 40 hours.

Atlas Software uses flexible working hours. Employees may start their day at any time between 08:00 and 10:00; in that case the end of the working day shifts so that the daily working time adds up to eight hours. However, 10:00 to 16:00 is core working time, and all employees are expected to be online or in the office during this period.

Overtime may only be worked with the written approval of a manager. Approved overtime hours are paid in the payroll at the hourly wage plus fifty percent. Instead of overtime pay, the employee may choose to take one and a half hours of time off for each hour worked.

Offices are closed on public holidays. When on-call work is needed on a public holiday, the on-call schedule is announced by team leads at least two weeks in advance.

3. Leave

Paid annual leave is 18 working days per year for employees who have not yet completed their first five years at the company. For employees who have completed five years at the company, annual leave increases to 24 working days. Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year; carried-over days are forfeited if they are not used by March 31.

Annual leave requests must be submitted to the manager through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the leave starts. Leave longer than five working days also requires the approval of the team lead.

Every employee receives 1 day of paid "birthday leave" on their birthday. If the birthday falls on a weekend, the leave can be used on the first working day after it.

Marriage leave is 5 working days and paternity leave is 10 working days. In the event of the death of a first-degree relative, the employee is granted 5 working days of paid leave. For sick leave, the doctor's report must be submitted to the HR department on the same day.

4. Remote Work

Atlas Software has adopted a hybrid working model. Employees may work remotely for at most 3 days a week; on the other days of the week they are expected to be in the office. Tuesday is designated as the "shared day" on which all teams are in the office, and remote work is not allowed on that day.

Requests for fully remote work are evaluated only in exceptional cases. Such a request must be submitted in writing to the manager at least two weeks before the start date and must be approved by the HR department.

Employees may work remotely from abroad for a total of at most 30 days per year. While working from abroad, the company VPN connection must be kept on at all times, and customer data may only be accessed from company devices.

Employees who work remotely receive a monthly internet and electricity allowance of 1,200 TL as a contribution to home office costs. This allowance is paid together with the salary, on the condition that the employee worked remotely for at least 8 days in that month.

5. Benefits

All employees receive a meal card top-up of 450 TL for each working day. Meal card top-ups continue on days worked remotely.

The company provides private health insurance for its employees. The health insurance also covers the employee's spouse and children under 18. Dental treatment is covered up to a limit of 6,000 TL per year.

The health insurance policy starts on the first day of the month following the employee's start date and is renewed every January. No co-payment is charged for outpatient treatment at contracted hospitals covered by the policy; at non-contracted institutions, eighty percent of the expenses are reimbursed, provided that the invoice and the doctor's report are sent to the insurance company. Glasses and contact lens expenses are covered up to 2,500 TL once every two years. Pregnancy and childbirth expenses are covered after a nine-month waiting period from the start of the policy. Employees who want to add family members to or remove them from the policy can only submit this request during the January renewal period; events such as marriage and birth are an exception to this rule and are processed during the policy period if they are reported within 30 days of the event.

For staff working at the Izmir headquarters, a free shuttle service runs in the morning and evening on the Karsiyaka, Bornova and Buca routes. Shuttle times and routes are published on the HR portal at the start of each quarter. No transportation allowance is paid to employees who do not use the shuttle; however, the company's partner card system can be used for monthly top-ups of public transport cards.

Every year, employees are given a training budget for personal development. This budget is 15,000 TL per person per year and can be used for conference attendance, online courses, certification exams and professional books. An employee who wants to use the budget submits a request form to their manager briefly explaining how the training relates to their work. After the manager approves it, the request goes to the HR department, and HR pays the training provider directly. If the employee has paid out of pocket, the payment is refunded with the next salary, provided that the invoice is submitted. Unused training budget does not carry over to the next year and is reset at the end of the year. There is an important rule for certifications obtained with the training budget: an employee who receives support of more than 10,000 TL from the budget for a single training or certification commits to working at the company for at least 12 months after the training ends. If the employee leaves voluntarily before this period ends, they repay the part of the training cost proportional to the remaining time. This commitment is signed separately on the training request form and kept in the employee's personnel file. In addition, internal technical trainings and mentoring programs are not deducted from this budget; they are free for all employees and are delivered during the "Atlas Academy" week held once every quarter.

Employees receive a monthly allowance of 750 TL for gym membership. To use the allowance, the membership invoice must be uploaded to the HR portal every month.

6. Expenses and Equipment

Business expenses must be entered in the expense portal, together with the invoice, no later than 30 days after the date of the expense. Expenses submitted after 30 days are not reimbursed.

Taxi costs for employees who leave the office after 23:00 are covered by the company. Travel costs for customer visits are also claimed through the same portal.

Every employee is given a laptop and an external monitor when they start. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. If equipment is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the IT department within 24 hours.

Installing personal software on company equipment is prohibited. Required software is installed by opening a request with the IT department.

7. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and December. A review consists of the employee's self-assessment, the manager's assessment and feedback from at least two teammates.

Based on the result of the December review, employees are paid an annual performance bonus. The bonus amount is set between 0 and 20 percent of the employee's gross monthly salary and is paid with the February salary.

For an employee who falls below expectations in two consecutive reviews, a six-month development plan is prepared. The employee's manager and HR business partner are jointly responsible for following up on the development plan.

Promotion decisions are announced only in January, after the December review.

8. Leaving the Company and Contact

An employee who wants to leave the company must comply with the notice periods. At our company, the notice period is 4 weeks for employees with less than one year of service, 6 weeks for those with one to three years, and 8 weeks for those with more than three years.

Before the last working day, the HR department holds an exit interview with the departing employee. On their last working day, the employee returns all company equipment issued to them to the IT department.
